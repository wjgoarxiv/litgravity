# Icon marks

An icon supports a label and sits beside it. A small, consistent set of simple marks lets a reader recognize a role at a glance without decoding a logo catalog.

## Selection

- A plain noun label comes first whenever space permits.
- Pick the icon by meaning. Visual resemblance alone is a weak reason.
- One role keeps one icon throughout a diagram.
- A brand mark always comes with a text label, because silhouette and brand color cannot identify a system by themselves.
- An unnamed organization or vendor gets a geometric placeholder. Logos are never invented.

## Drawing

- Use a 20 to 24px viewBox and a consistent stroke of about 1.5px at that size.
- Icon paths are single-color with `currentColor`, taking the diagram's ink or muted token.
- Align icons to the label baseline and keep at least 8px of clear space around them.
- Filled marks are for source icons that were designed as filled silhouettes.
- Hide a redundant decorative icon from assistive technology and keep the adjacent visible text name.

`assets/icons.html` shows the shipped marks with their source groups. `NOTICE` records Tabler, Simple Icons, log-z/logos, Devicon, one-off sources and the trademark restrictions. Every icon added to the sheet needs a provenance entry.
