# Summary of changes

From Monday 2 November to Friday 11 December, the contractor will replace the roof membrane above the third floor. The third-floor kitchen closes for the whole period because its ceiling is opened for pipework. Two temporary lunch rooms open on the second floor, loud work stays inside two fixed windows each day, and the east stairwell becomes the only route to the roof hatch. Desks on the third floor stay in use.

# Reasons for the work

The membrane is 22 years old and leaked in three places during the September storms, one of them over the kitchen cabinets. The surveyor advised that a patch would not last the winter. The kitchen pipework runs under the section of roof being replaced, so the plumber needs the room empty for six weeks (sample figure).

# What changes for you

## Lunch and drinks

The two temporary rooms on the second floor, Meeting Room 2A and the old print room, hold 36 seats between them against 54 in the kitchen (sample figures). Microwaves, kettles and two fridges move with them. Label your food; the fridges are cleared at 17:00 every Friday.

Hot water is available at the drinks point by reception. Bring a lidded bottle if you fill up at the third-floor cooler, because the corridor floor stays covered with protective sheeting until 11 December and spills are hard to clear from it.

## Noise windows

Hammering and cutting happen from 09:30 to 11:30 and from 13:30 to 15:30 only. Calls and workshops that need quiet should sit outside those hours. The contractor posts the day's plan at each stairwell door by 08:30.

## Deliveries and fire safety

Parcels for the third floor go to reception during the works, and the front desk will message you when one arrives. The fire alarm test moves from Wednesday morning to Thursday at 14:00 on the weeks the scaffold is up, so that crews can stand down safely. Floor wardens receive the revised walking route at their briefing on 29 October.

## Access to the roof hatch

Only the east stairwell leads to the hatch. The west stairwell stays clear for people leaving the building. Keep the east landings clear of bags and parcels, because crews carry material through them from 08:00.
