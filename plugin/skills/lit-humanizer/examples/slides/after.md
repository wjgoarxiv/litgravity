# Summer reading programme review

## 1. Scope
1 July to 31 August 2026, 12 primary schools

## 2. Participation
1,340 pupils signed up; 1,012 finished at least five books

## 3. Cost
9,600 for books and prizes, about 7.16 per pupil who signed up

## 4. Teacher feedback
9 of 12 schools returned the form; 7 asked for more books at lower reading levels

## 5. Next step
The council decides on 2027 funding at its February meeting
