Without further ado, here are the slides for the reading programme review.

## Slide 1: Overview
The summer reading programme ran from 1 July to 31 August 2026 in 12 primary schools.

## Slide 2: Participation
1,340 pupils signed up, and 1,012 finished at least five books.

## Slide 3: Key Takeaway
The programme stands as a testament to the power of reading.

## Slide 4: Costs
Books and prizes cost 9,600 in total, about 7.16 per pupil who signed up.

## Slide 5: Feedback
Teachers in 9 of the 12 schools returned the feedback form. Seven asked for more books at lower reading levels.

## Slide 6: Next Steps
The council will decide on 2027 funding at its February meeting.
