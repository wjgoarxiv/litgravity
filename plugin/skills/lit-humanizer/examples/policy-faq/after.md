# Remote work FAQ

**Q: How many days a week can I work from home?**
Up to two days a week, if your manager agrees.

**Q: Do I need to use a company laptop?**
Yes. Personal devices cannot connect to the internal network.

**Q: Is there an allowance for home office equipment?**
If you work from home at least one day a week, you can claim up to 300 once every three years. Keep your receipts; every claim needs them.

**Q: Can I work from home in another country?**
For up to 10 working days a year, with written approval from HR. Ask at least two weeks in advance; working abroad raises tax and insurance questions that HR has to check.

**Q: What if my internet connection fails?**
Come to the office, or take the time as leave.
