# Remote work FAQ

**Q: How many days a week can I work from home?**
Great question! Staff can work from home up to two days a week, subject to their manager's agreement.

**Q: Do I need to use a company laptop?**
Yes. Personal devices cannot connect to the internal network. This isn't just a security rule; it's a commitment to protecting our data.

**Q: Is there an allowance for home office equipment?**
Staff who work from home at least one day a week can claim up to 300 once every three years. Receipts are required.

**Q: Can I work from home in another country?**
It is important to note that working from abroad raises complex tax and insurance questions. Staff may work from another country for up to 10 working days a year, and only with written approval from HR at least two weeks in advance.

**Q: What if my internet connection fails?**
Based on the provided information, staff should come to the office or take the time as leave.
