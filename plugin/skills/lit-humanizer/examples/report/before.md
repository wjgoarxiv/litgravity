## Background

In today's world, sustainability is more important than ever. The pilot placed sorting bins for paper, plastic and food waste on four floors of the municipal office from 3 March to 30 May 2026.

## Findings

Contamination in the recycling bins fell from 31% in the first month to 18% in the third month, based on weekly audits by the cleaning contractor. Food waste collected rose from 42 kg to 66 kg per week. These results highlight the transformative potential of simple behavioural changes.

## Costs

The bins, signs and two training sessions for floor wardens cost 2,350 in total. Moreover, the cleaning contractor charged no extra fee for the weekly audits, as they were included in the existing contract. This represents a pivotal opportunity to scale the pilot cost-effectively.

## Limitations

Audits not verified independently; not a legal decision basis. The pilot covered one building, so the figures may not apply to other sites.
