# Waste sorting pilot, municipal office

From 3 March to 30 May 2026, the pilot placed bins for paper, plastic and food waste on four floors of the municipal office.

The cleaning contractor audited the bins every week. Contamination in the recycling bins fell from 31% in the first month to 18% in the third. Food waste collected rose from 42 kg to 66 kg a week.

The bins, signs and two training sessions for floor wardens cost 2,350 in total. The weekly audits cost nothing extra; the existing cleaning contract already covered them.

Only the contractor checked the audits, and the pilot covered one building, so other sites may see different figures. The results give no basis for a legal decision.
