# Printer error E-41

E-41 means the paper tray sensor cannot detect paper. It usually happens after the tray is overfilled.

1. Open the tray.
2. Remove paper until the stack is below the MAX line (about 250 sheets).
3. Close the tray firmly.

If the error stays, the sensor may be dirty. Wipe the small window at the back of the tray with a dry cloth. Water and cleaning spray can damage the sensor, so use the dry cloth only.

If E-41 appears with a correctly loaded tray, open the printer's settings page and check the firmware version. Versions older than 4.2 show E-41 by mistake; download the update from the internal software portal.

If the error still appears, call the help desk at extension 4410.
