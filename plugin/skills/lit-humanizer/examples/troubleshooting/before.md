Great question! If the printer shows error E-41, here's what you need to know.

Error E-41 means the paper tray sensor cannot detect paper. It is worth noting that this usually happens after the tray is overfilled.

Step 1: Open the tray.
Step 2: Remove paper until the stack is below the line marked MAX (about 250 sheets).
Step 3: Close the tray firmly.

If the error persists, the sensor may be dirty. Wipe the small window at the back of the tray with a dry cloth. Do not use water or cleaning spray, because they can damage the sensor.

If the error appears with a full, correctly loaded tray, check the firmware version on the printer's settings page. Versions older than 4.2 are known to show E-41 by mistake; the update can be downloaded from the internal software portal.

If none of this works, contact the help desk at extension 4410. I hope this helps!
