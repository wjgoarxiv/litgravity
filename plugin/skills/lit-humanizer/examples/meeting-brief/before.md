**Sources:** the facilities team's October report and the September member survey.

The meeting will decide whether to move the community garden's tool shed from the north gate to the east path. It is important to note that the move would cost about 1,800, according to the facilities team's estimate. Moreover, 41 of the 57 members who answered the September survey said the east path would be easier to reach.

Two options are on the table. Option A moves the shed in March, before the planting season. Option B waits until October, after the harvest, which avoids disturbing the beds near the east path but leaves members with the longer walk for another season.

Experts say that relocations like this can potentially improve participation. The decision will need to be made before the facilities budget closes on 15 November.
