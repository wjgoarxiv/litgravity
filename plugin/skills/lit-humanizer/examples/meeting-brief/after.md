# Brief for Thursday's planning meeting

The meeting decides whether to move the community garden's tool shed from the north gate to the east path.

The facilities team's October report estimates the move at about 1,800. In the September member survey, 41 of the 57 members who answered said the east path would be easier to reach. Moving the shed might also raise participation; the brief has no figures on that.

There are two options. Moving the shed in March (option A) finishes before the planting season. Waiting until October (option B) avoids disturbing the beds near the east path during the harvest, but members keep the longer walk for another season.

The facilities budget closes on 15 November, so the decision has to be made before then.
