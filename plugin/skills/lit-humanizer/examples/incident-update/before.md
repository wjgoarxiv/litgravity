Certainly! Here is the latest on the payment page outage.

At 14:05 local time the payment page began returning errors for card payments. Bank transfers were not affected. The cause was an expired certificate on the connection to the card processor. Our team identified the root cause and worked diligently to resolve it.

The certificate was renewed at 14:52, and card payments resumed at 14:55. Between 14:05 and 14:55, 312 card payments failed; no customer was charged for a failed payment. We are monitoring the situation closely.

The failed payments were not lost. Customers who saw an error can simply retry the payment, and all orders placed during the outage were kept in the basket for 24 hours. Going forward, we will add an automatic alert 30 days before any certificate expires, which underscores our commitment to reliability.

As of my last update, no further errors have been reported. I hope this helps!
