## Q4 options: hire 40 agents vs self-service first

:::: columns 1fr 1fr
::: col
- **Plan A: hire 40 agents**
  (1) Capacity: adds 4k tickets a day, headroom to Q4 2027
  (2) Cost: $2.1m in year one, then $1.8m a year to run
  (3) Reply time: median stays at 6 min, peaks near 15 min
  (4) Timing: fully staffed in March 2027, 4 months of hiring and training
:::
::: col
- **Plan B: self-service first**
  (1) Capacity: the app carries 35% of tickets by March 2027, desk lasts to Q4 2027
  (2) Cost: $0.9m in year one, then $0.3m a year to run
  (3) Reply time: median falls to 4 min in every channel
  (4) Timing: first release in January 2027, hiring moves to a study
:::
::::

- Source: Example Care Operations cost and capacity estimates, September 2026
