# Three small libraries, one shared budget
---

---
layout: text-two-column

## Brief and scope of the three-branch renovation

- **The brief**
  (1) Three neighbourhood libraries, 1,600 square metres in all, 210,000 visits a year
  (2) Built between 1988 and 1996, each under 600 square metres
  (3) One shared budget and a promise to keep each branch open most of the year
  (4) Reading rooms that had become storage for furniture nobody used
- **What we changed**
  (1) Opened the street walls with deep window seats
  (2) Moved shelving to the edges so the floor reads as one room
  (3) Kept every original structural wall, which halved the permit time
  (4) Added one accessible entrance per branch, level with the pavement
- **Budget and programme**
  (1) One shared budget of €2.4m, spent €2.31m, 4% under
  (2) Works fifteen weeks in all, one branch at a time, so two stayed open
  (3) Reused 64 pieces of furniture, saving about €0.18m on new fittings
- Source: Example Architecture Studio project records, 2025-2026 (example)

## Floor use and seating before and after

:::: columns 1fr 1fr
::: col
- **Before**
  (1) Shelving: 41% of the floor, much of it half-empty bays
  (2) Daylight: one room per branch, the rest lit all day by ceiling panels
  (3) Seating: 46 readers per branch on average, mostly at long tables
  (4) Access: one step-free entrance across all three branches
:::
::: col
- **After**
  (1) Shelving: 24% of the floor, holding the same number of books
  (2) Daylight: reaches every reading table through the opened street walls
  (3) Seating: 78 readers per branch on average (+32 seats), in four seat types
  (4) Access: a step-free entrance at every branch, level with the pavement
:::
::::

- Source: Example Architecture Studio measured surveys, spring 2025 and spring 2026 (example)

## Window seat section and occupancy

![Figure 4. Window seat section and occupancy | Source: example image](assets/example-c.png)

- **Use** the most used seats in every branch
- **Depth** 900 mm, enough to sit sideways with a laptop
- **Light** north-facing glass, so the seats stay usable in summer
- **Occupancy** 71% of opening hours, against 38% for tables
- **Count** 22 window seats across the three branches
- **Cost** about €1,900 per seat in oak and felt, built by one local joiner
- Source: Example occupancy counts, hourly, March-May 2026 (example)
