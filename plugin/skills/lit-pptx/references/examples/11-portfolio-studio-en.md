# Four neighbourhood bakeries, one shared fit-out
---

---
layout: text-two-column

## Brief and scope of the four-shop fit-out

- **The brief**
  (1) Four neighbourhood bakery shops, 380 square metres in all, 5,300 sales a week
  (2) Fitted out between 2009 and 2014, each under 120 square metres
  (3) One shared budget and a promise to keep three shops trading at any time
  (4) Counters that had grown into storage for trays nobody used
- **What we changed**
  (1) Opened the shopfronts with deep window benches
  (2) Moved shelving to the walls so the floor reads as one room
  (3) Kept every original oven flue and floor drain, which cut each closure by a third
  (4) Added one step-free entrance per shop, level with the pavement
- **Budget and programme**
  (1) One shared budget of €1.2m, spent €1.14m, 5% under
  (2) Works eleven weeks in all, one shop at a time, so three stayed open
  (3) Reused 38 pieces of equipment, saving about €0.09m on new fittings
- Source: Example Interior Studio project records, 2025-2026 (example)

## Window bench section and occupancy

![Figure 4. Window bench section and occupancy | Source: example image](assets/example-c.png)

- **Use** the most used seats in every shop
- **Depth** 550 mm, enough for a tray and a coffee beside a seated customer
- **Light** south-facing glass with an outside awning, so the seats stay usable in summer
- **Occupancy** 78% of opening hours, against 41% for tables
- **Count** 26 window seats across the four shops
- **Cost** about €1,400 per seat in ash and wool felt, built by one local joiner
- Source: Example occupancy counts, hourly, March-May 2026 (example)
